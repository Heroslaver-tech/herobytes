TableHarvest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

TableHarvest is a Chrome extension that exports tables from web pages to the clipboard, CSV, Excel (.xlsx) and Google Sheets. It was designed so that your data never has to leave your computer.

1. What the extension reads

The extension reads the content of the tables on the page you are viewing only when you ask it to: by clicking the toolbar icon, choosing "Export this table" in the right-click menu, or, if you enable it in Settings, automatically on every page. The table data is processed in your browser to build the preview and the exported file. It is not sent to us or to any third party.

2. What the extension stores

On your device, using Chrome's extension storage, the extension keeps:

  • Your preferences: CSV separator, encoding, file name pattern, header and number options.
  • If you buy TableHarvest Pro: your license key, its activation status and the date of the last check.
  • If you use multi-page capture: the rows collected so far, kept only for the current browser session.
  • If you connect Google Sheets: a short-lived access token issued by Google.

Chrome may sync preferences across your devices if you use Chrome sync. Nothing is stored on our servers; we have none.

3. Network requests

The extension makes no network requests during normal use. The only exceptions are:

  • Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), our payment and licensing provider, when you activate a Pro license key and periodically afterwards to confirm it is still valid. The request contains your license key and a device label such as "Chrome on Windows". Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself: lemonsqueezy.com/privacy.
  • Google Sheets API (sheets.googleapis.com), only if you explicitly connect your Google account in Settings to create spreadsheets directly. The spreadsheet content is sent to your own Google account. You can disconnect at any time. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. What we do not do

We do not collect browsing history, page content, personal information, analytics or crash reports. We do not use cookies or tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any. The extension contains no remotely loaded code.

5. Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting: to read the tables of the current page after your click.
  • contextMenus: the right-click entry.
  • storage: preferences and license, stored locally.
  • Optional, requested only if you turn the feature on: access to all sites (automatic badges), api.lemonsqueezy.com (license activation), identity (Google Sheets connection).

6. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Material changes will be mentioned in the extension's release notes.
