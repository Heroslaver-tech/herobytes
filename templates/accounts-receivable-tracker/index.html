What an aging report tells you

The one table that shows where your cash is stuck.

An accounts receivable aging report groups every unpaid invoice by how late it is. Money in "current" is fine. Money in "31-60" needs a reminder. Money in "90+" is the money you may never see. Small businesses and freelancers usually keep this in their head or in the invoicing tool; this template keeps it in one spreadsheet you control, and recalculates it for any date you choose.

  1. Settings: your business name, currency code, default payment terms (for example 30 days) and the report date. Leave the report date empty to always see today.
  2. Clients: one row per client, with their payment terms and an optional credit limit.
  3. Invoices: one row per invoice: number, client, issue date and amount. The due date, paid amount, balance, status, days overdue and aging bucket are calculated.
  4. Payments: one row per payment with the invoice number. Partial payments are fine; a check warns you if the invoice number does not exist.
  5. Dashboard and Statement: the Dashboard shows the totals, an aging chart and the five most overdue invoices to chase first. On Statement, pick a client to get their open invoices and balance due, ready to print or save as PDF and send.

Can I do this with a blank spreadsheet?

Yes: a payments total per invoice with SUMIF, a due date from the invoice or client terms, days overdue against the report date, and a COUNTIFS and SUMIFS per bucket. The hard parts are partial payments, terms that come from three places, and formulas that do not break in Excel with another language or decimal comma. The template has all of that done and tested in Excel and Google Sheets.

Questions

Which Excel versions does it work in?

Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, on Windows and Mac, in any language and with a decimal point or a decimal comma.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. The download includes a PDF with a link that makes your own copy in Google Sheets in one click.

Does it create or send invoices?

No. It tracks the invoices you already issue with your invoicing tool or accountant, and tells you what is owed and how late.
